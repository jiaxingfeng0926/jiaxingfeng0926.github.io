**Discovering ESLint and Coding Standards**

At first, I had no idea what ESLint was or what exactly coding standards meant.  When I first encountered ESLint, it seemed overwhelming and confusing.  I remember looking at all the warnings and errors and not understanding why they mattered or how to fix them.  Coding standards, which are meant to make code consistent and readable, initially felt like extra rules that slowed me down.  I faced many problems during this period, and even now, I do not fully understand everything about ESLint or all coding standards.  However, this learning expericence was very important for me and it has taught me to approach coding more carefully.

**Challenges I Faced**

One of the main challenges I encountered was compiling TypeScript to JavaScript properly.  I could not figure out why my code wasn’t working as expected, and this took a long time to resolve.  Sometimes the ESLint warnings seemed unrelated to the problem I was trying to solve, which made me frustrated.  I often felt stuck, unsure if I was making progress.  However, these challenges forced me to slow down, read documentation more carefully, and experiment with different solutions. After that I realized all these difficulties were not obstacles but opportunities to improve my skills.
  
**Learning from Coding Standards**

Using coding standards has been instrumental in developing my skills as a collaborative programmer. By emphasizing clarity and consistency, standards like those enforced by ESLint have trained me to be more meticulous with details—from how I name variables to how I structure functions. ESLint acts as a mentor, pointing out pitfalls and promoting best practices. This focus on writing clean code has made my work more robust and understandable, while also giving me the confidence to effectively read and contribute to larger codebases.

**Conclusion**

In conclusion, working with ESLint and learning about coding standards has been a challenging yet highly rewarding experience.  This process has taught me patience and persistence while underscoring the critical importance of writing clear, consistent code.  These experiences have not only sharpened my technical abilities but have also encouraged me to think more critically about the quality and readability of my work.
