**Estimation Approach and Initial Assumptions**

Effort estimation played an important role in how I planned and evaluated my work on the UH Connect project. At the beginning of the project, we created effort estimates by breaking features into individual GitHub issues and assigning estimated minutes based on the perceived complexity of each task. Simpler UI-related tasks, such as creating static pages or updating navigation, were estimated with lower time values, while backend-oriented tasks like database structure, Prisma integration, and admin functionality were estimated higher. My estimates were based mainly on experience from earlier ICS 314 assignments and my familiarity with Next.js, React, and database-related work.

**Value of Estimation Despite Inaccuracy**

Although many of my estimates ended up being inaccurate, estimating in advance still provided clear benefits. Creating estimates forced me to think through implementation details before starting work. For example, tasks involving database access or admin-only features initially appeared straightforward, but estimating effort made me consider authentication logic, schema changes, and potential deployment issues.    Even when tasks took significantly longer than expected, the estimation process helped me identify which tasks were likely to be more complex and required more careful planning.

**Effort Tracking Approach and Improvements**

we tracked my actual effort using GitHub Issues along with manual time tracking. After completing each issue, I recorded approximate time spent on coding and non-coding activities such as planning, debugging, reading documentation, and writing setup guides. Although this approach was not perfectly accurate since some time was estimated after the fact, it was consistent enough to reveal meaningful patterns in my work. If I were to repeat this project, I would improve my process by explicitly accounting for debugging and integration time in my estimates, breaking larger tasks into smaller issues, and tracking time closer to real time to increase accuracy and reliability.
